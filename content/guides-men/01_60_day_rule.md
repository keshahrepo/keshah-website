## What's actually happening on your head

Notice something? Feel the top of your scalp. Now feel the sides above your ears.

The top is tight. The sides move freely. Most men go bald in a pattern that exactly traces where the scalp is tightest — the crown, the temples, the front. The sides and back (where scalp tissue stays loose) keep their hair through life.

This isn't a coincidence. It's the mechanism.

When your scalp tightens, it compresses the blood vessels that feed your hair follicles. Restricted blood flow means follicles get less oxygen, less nutrients, and slowly produce thinner, weaker hair. Eventually they stop producing visible hair entirely.

DHT is real — but its damaging effects are dramatically amplified when blood flow is already restricted. Fix the blood flow, and DHT becomes far less powerful.

Here's the actual protocol:

1. **Start the daily routine today.** Don't change anything else.

## What to expect along the way

**Days 1–3**: Your scalp may feel a little sore after sessions. Some men feel tingling — that's blood circulation returning to areas that have been blocked. Both are good signs.

**Days 3–7**: Your scalp will start to feel less tight. You'll be able to move it more easily under your fingers. This is the first real signal the protocol is working.

**Days 7–14**: You may notice *more* hair falling out than usual. **This is normal and a good sign.** When circulation returns to follicles, your scalp pushes out the weakest hairs — the ones that were dying anyway. Think of it like clearing dead trees before new growth.

**Days 30–60**: For most men, this is when shedding noticeably slows. Hair fall reduces. Some start to see baby hairs filling in at the hairline or temples.

**Day 60**: Hair fall has generally stopped for most men. If it hasn't, that's your signal to dig deeper or add other tools.

## The trade-offs of the drug route

Just so you have the honest picture for after day 60:

**Minoxidil**:
- Works while you use it. Stop, and you shed harder than before.
- Costs $5,000–$10,000+ over 30 years.
- Side effects: scalp irritation, possible heart palpitations, ankle swelling, sleep disruption.

- Works by blocking DHT systemically (not just on your scalp).
- Side effects: libido changes, mood changes, "post-finasteride syndrome" in a small percentage of users (sometimes persistent after stopping).
- Lifetime commitment to keep gains.

Both are real options. Both have real costs. The KESHAH protocol is just a different option — one that doesn't have ongoing chemical exposure or lifetime drug dependence.
